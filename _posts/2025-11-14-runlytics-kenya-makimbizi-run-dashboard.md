## Project Overview
This project involves creating a **Tableau dashboard for Runlytics Kenya** to analyze the Makimbizi Run with simulated and realistic data. Inspired by the Standard Chartered Nairobi Marathon, the dashboard includes:  
- **Race Categories**: 5km, 10km, 21km, 42km  
- **Demographics**: Gender, Age Group  
- **Regions/Counties**: Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru, Meru  
- **Metrics**: Participants, Average Speed, Race Duration, Satisfaction, Social Media Engagement  
- **Analysis Dimensions**: Race category impact, performance trends, regional participation, social engagement  

The dashboard allows stakeholders to **identify trends, top-performing race categories, and regional participation**, helping them make informed decisions to **improve event planning, marketing, and participant satisfaction**.

---

## Objectives
- Visualize participation and performance trends across race categories and demographics  
- Analyze **regional participation** and social engagement metrics  
- Highlight **race categories with highest engagement and satisfaction**  
- Demonstrate Tableau skills including:  
  - Heatmaps for performance by gender and age  
  - Bar charts for participation and satisfaction  
  - Scatter plots for speed vs duration  
  - KPI cards for key metrics  
  - Filters by Race Category, Gender, Age Group, County, Year  

---

## Tools & Techniques
- **Tableau Public** for dashboard creation and interactive visualization  
- **Key Metrics Visualized**:  
  - Total Participants  
  - Average Race Duration  
  - Average Satisfaction  
  - Average Speed  
  - Social Media Engagement by Race  
  - Regional Participation  
- **Interactivity**: Filters and hover tooltips for dynamic insights

## Filters
- Race Category, Gender, Age Group, County, Year

## Recommendations
1. **Focus marketing on popular races** to increase participation in underrepresented categories  
2. **Target low-participation regions** to improve overall turnout  
3. **Leverage social engagement trends** to drive race awareness  
4. **Optimize race scheduling and participant experience** based on performance trends and satisfaction metrics
