## Project Overview
This project involves analyzing a **synthetic KPLC electricity dataset** with 500 sample entries. The dataset includes:  
- **Customer IDs** representing unique electricity consumers  
- **Regions** including Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Meru  
- **Units Consumed (kWh)** per customer  
- **Payment Status**: Paid or Pending  
- **Billing Periods / Months**  
- **Payments (KES)** per customer  

The analysis provides insights into: **total revenue, consumption trends, top consumers, payment patterns, and regional differences**, supporting better operational and financial planning.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze electricity consumption and payment trends across regions and months.  
- Demonstrate data analysis and dashboarding skills including:  
  - **Aggregations**: SUM, AVG, COUNT per customer, region, or month  
  - **Top Consumer Analysis**: Identify the highest energy users  
  - **Payment Analysis**: Track paid vs pending bills  
  - **Trend Analysis**: Monthly consumption patterns  
- Generate actionable insights for energy usage and revenue monitoring.

---

## Tools & Techniques
- **Excel** for data cleaning, aggregation, and pivot tables  
- **Pivot Charts & Slicers** for interactive dashboards  
- **Conditional Formatting & KPI Cards** for visualization  
- **Key Metrics Analyzed:**  
  - Total revenue and units consumed  
  - Average bill per customer  
  - Payment status distribution  
  - Top 10 consumers by units and payments  
  - Monthly consumption trends by region  

---

## Key Steps & Analysis Highlights

### 1. Data Loading & Cleaning
- Imported the KPLC dataset with customer, region, units, and payment data  
- Handled missing values and ensured consistent formatting  
- Verified numeric fields (Units, Payments) for accuracy  

### 2. Dashboard & Visualization
- **Total Revenue & Units:** KPI cards summarizing totals and averages  
- **Monthly Consumption Trend:** Line chart to show usage over time  
- **Payment Status Distribution:** Pie chart of Paid vs Pending bills  
- **Top 10 Consumers:** Sorted table with conditional formatting bars  
- **Regional Analysis:** Bar charts comparing revenue and consumption by region  

### 3. Interactive Features
- **Slicers** added for Region, Month, and Payment Status  
- Dashboards dynamically update to filter by region or billing period  

---

## Key Insights
1. **Revenue leaders:** Certain regions contribute most to total revenue (e.g., Nairobi and Mombasa).  
2. **Top consumers:** A small percentage of customers account for high energy usage.  
3. **Payment trends:** Majority of bills are paid on time, but some regions show higher pending rates.  
4. **Monthly patterns:** Consumption peaks during specific months, highlighting seasonal demand.  
5. **Operational insights:** Dashboard allows quick identification of high-usage customers and regions needing attention.
