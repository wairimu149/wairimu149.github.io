## Project Overview  
This project involves creating a **synthetic student residence dataset** with 700 sample entries. The dataset includes:  
- **Residences** across different halls in Kenya  
- **Students** with ID, age, gender, and program  
- **Room Types**: Single, Double, Triple  
- **Bookings** including successful and cancelled tickets  
- **Payment Methods**: MPESA (dominant), ATM, Cash, Cheque  
- **Booking Seasons**: Normal, Holiday, New Semester, Exam Period  
- **Occupancy Status**: Occupied, Vacant  

The dataset provides insights into **room preferences, revenue trends, occupancy rates, and payment patterns**, helping residence management plan effectively.

---

## Objectives  
- Simulate a student residence dataset to analyze operational and financial metrics.  
- Demonstrate **data analysis and visualization skills in Excel**, including:
  - **Aggregations**: SUM, AVG, COUNT per room, year, or season  
  - **Filtering**: By payment method, room type, year of study 
  - **Time Analysis**: Trends over months or semesters  
- Generate actionable insights for **management, resource planning, and student services**.

---

## Key Insights  
1. **Room preferences:** 4th-year students mostly select Single rooms, followed by 3rd, 2nd, and 1st years.  
2. **Occupancy trends:** Peak during holidays and exam periods; weekdays are less occupied.  
3. **Revenue drivers:** Single rooms contribute the most to total revenue, followed by Double and Triple rooms.  
4. **Payment patterns:** MPESA dominates, followed by ATM, Cash, and Cheque.  
5. **Booking success rates:** Majority of bookings are successful; cancellations are concentrated around certain periods.  

---

## Recommendations  
- **Optimize room allocation:** Assign more Single rooms to higher-year students.  
- **Monitor occupancy:** Plan for peak seasons with additional resources.  
- **Promote alternative payment methods:** Encourage ATM or Cash for diversification.  
- **Revenue planning:** Focus on Single rooms for revenue maximization.  
- **Interactive dashboard:** Include slicers for Year of Study, Room Type, Payment Method, Season, and Occupancy Status to enhance decision-making.
