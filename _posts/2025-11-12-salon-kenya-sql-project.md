## Project Overview  
This project involves creating a **simulated salon database** in MySQL with 300+ sample entries. The database includes:  
- **Branches** across different regions in Kenya  
- **Employees** including hair stylists and nail technicians  
- **Clients** and their bookings  
- **Services** such as hair, nails, and other salon offerings  
- **Products** sold in branches  
- **Payments** via MPESA, Cash, ATM, Cheque  
- **Marketing channels** including social media and in-branch boards  

The SQL queries provide insights into: **top services, branch revenue, employee performance, client activity, and payment patterns**.

---

## Objectives  
- Create and load a MySQL database for a multi-branch salon business.  
- Demonstrate SQL skills including:
  - **JOINs** between bookings, services, products, and employees  
  - **Aggregations**: SUM, AVG, COUNT per branch or category  
  - **Subqueries**: Identify top clients or best-performing employees  
  - **Filters**: Analyze data by payment method, region, or time period  
- Generate actionable insights for business and marketing decisions.

## Tools & Techniques  
- **MySQL / MySQL Workbench** for database creation and query execution  
- **SQL Queries** for data analysis including:  
  - Aggregations: SUM, AVG, COUNT

  - Subqueries for top performers  
  - Filters by region, payment method, and time

---

## Key Steps & Query Highlights

### 1. Database Creation & Data Loading
- Created tables: `branches`, `employees`, `clients`, `services`, `bookings`, `payments`, `products`, `product_sales`, `marketing`  
- Inserted 300+ realistic sample entries across all tables  
- Verified data integrity with primary and foreign keys

### 2. SQL Analysis Queries
- **Revenue per service:** Total revenue generated by each salon service  
- **Top-performing employees:** Number of bookings handled and revenue generated  
- **Top clients:** Clients with highest spending on bookings  
- **Branch revenue:** Aggregated revenue from bookings and product sales  
- **Most popular services:** Top 10 most booked services  
- **Filters:** Analyze bookings by payment method (MPESA, Cash, ATM, Cheque) and region  

---

## Key Insights  
1. **Revenue leaders:** Certain services (e.g., hair styling, nail treatments) generate the highest revenue per branch.  
2. **Employee performance:** Stylists and nail technicians differ in total bookings handled; top performers drive most revenue.  
3. **Client behavior:** A few loyal clients account for the majority of bookings and product purchases.  
4. **Payment patterns:** MPESA is the most commonly used payment method, followed by Cash and ATM.  
5. **Service popularity:** Nail and hair services dominate bookings; some services are underbooked, suggesting marketing opportunities.
