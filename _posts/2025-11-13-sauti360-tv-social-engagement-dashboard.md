## Project Overview
This project involves creating a **Tableau dashboard for Sauti360** to analyze TV and social media engagement over time with simulated and realistic data. The dashboard includes:  
- **Platforms**: YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X  
- **Regions**: Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru, Meru  
- **Events**: Elections, COVID-19 Updates, Protests, None  
- **Metrics**: Views, Likes, Comments, Shares, Engagement Rate, TV Viewership  
- **Analysis Dimensions**: Event impact, platform performance, regional reach  

The dashboard allows stakeholders to **identify trends, high-performing platforms, and event-driven engagement spikes**, helping them make informed decisions to **grow viewership and revenue**.

---

## Objectives
- Visualize engagement trends across platforms and regions for Sauti360  
- Assess the **impact of specific events** on TV and social engagement  
- Provide insights to improve **viewership and revenue**  
- Demonstrate Tableau skills including:  
  - Dual-axis line charts for trend analysis  
  - Stacked and clustered bar charts for comparative insights  
  - Scatter plots for event intensity vs engagement  
  - KPI cards for key metrics overview  
  - Filters by Platform, Region, Event, and Date

## Dashboard Structure

### Top Section
- **Horizontal Container:** KPI Cards  
  - Total Views  
  - Average Engagement Rate  
  - Total TV Viewership  
  - Top Platform by Engagement  
  - Top Event by Engagement  
  - **Heading:** “TV & Social Engagement Overview”

### Middle Section
- **Vertical Container:** Trend & Platform Analysis  
  - **Line Chart:** TV Viewership & Social Engagement Over Time  
  - **Stacked Bar Chart:** Engagement by Platform  
  - **Container Heading:** “Audience & Engagement Overview”

### Bottom Section
- **Horizontal Container:** Detailed Analysis  
  - **Bar Chart:** TV Viewership by Region  
    - **Heading:** “TV Viewership by Region”  
  - **Scatter Plot:** Event Impact on Engagement  
    - **Heading:** “Event Impact on Engagement”  

### Filters
- Platform, Region, Event, Date Range

## Recommendations for More Views and Revenue
1. **Promote high-impact events:** Allocate marketing budget to events that historically drive higher engagement (e.g., Elections, COVID-19 Updates).  
2. **Target underperforming platforms:** Invest in campaigns on platforms with lower engagement to balance audience distribution.  
3. **Regional marketing focus:** Focus on regions with lower TV viewership to increase total reach.  
4. **Cross-promotion:** Combine TV and social campaigns to amplify engagement across both channels.  
5. **Content timing optimization:** Schedule posts and broadcasts during peak engagement periods identified in the trends chart.
